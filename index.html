How it works

Transparent, fixed-scope sprints. You always know what you're paying for.

  1. Discovery

    We talk about your business, goals and scope, then agree on a plan together.

  2. Design

    Wireframe → visual design → your feedback, until it's exactly right.

  3. Build

    Weekly demos, clean tested code on a staging link you can check anytime.

  4. Launch

    Deploy to your domain, handover docs, and free bug support afterwards.

Questions clients ask

Short answers. If yours isn't here, message me.

How much does a project cost?

Cost depends on scope, so there is no single price. Tell me what you need and we agree on a plan together.

How long does it take?

WordPress/landing sites: 1–3 weeks. PWA web apps: 2–5 weeks. Telegram bots and automation: 3–10 days. I only take one or two projects at a time, so the schedule I give you is the schedule you get.

Do you work with clients outside Iran?

Yes — I work remotely worldwide. International clients pay with USDT (crypto), which is fast and reliable from anywhere. My hours overlap with both Europe and East Asia.

Can you handle both design and code?

Yes, one person from wireframe to deployment. If you already have a designer or a brand kit, I build to it exactly.

What happens after launch?

Every project ships with handover documentation, and bug fixes after launch are free. If you want ongoing work, I keep the staging link live and we continue sprint by sprint.

What if I don't know what I need?

That's the normal starting point. Tell me the business problem and the outcome you want; I'll propose the shape of the product, what it should include, and what can wait for version two.
