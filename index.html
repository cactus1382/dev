Marketplace · in production

HomeFoodPro — home-cooked food, ordered like takeaway

Customers order from home cooks and local shops nearby. Cooks run their kitchen from a panel, couriers are booked automatically, and money settles every night. I own all of it: product, design, code, servers and support.

Team
One developer — me

  • Live order tracking

    Status changes reach the customer and the cook the moment they happen, over Server-Sent Events. New orders ring a full-screen alert.

  • Payments and nightly payouts

    Zibal gateway, an in-app wallet and a nightly settlement list for every cook. Cash orders only deduct the commission.

  • Courier dispatch

    SnappBox price quotes and a background sync that moves orders to "delivering" and "delivered" on its own, with alerts on cancellations.

  • Trust and safety

    Cook identity checks, a weighted trust score that drives ranking, and a moderation queue for photos and reviews.

  • Native Android app

    Built with Capacitor from the same code, published on Myket, with Firebase push and an adaptive themed icon.

  • Run from Telegram

    A bot sends admins new-order alerts, payout checklists and support tickets, so daily operations fit in one chat.

  • Built to be found

    Twelve city pages, dish guides, schema.org markup and llms.txt, so both search engines and AI assistants can cite it.

  • Encrypted bank details

    Cooks' IBANs are encrypted at rest with a separate master key. Third-party errors never leak to the client.
